Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FinanceHub may collect, use and protect information when visitors use the site.

Information we collect

FinanceHub may collect information that visitors voluntarily provide, such as an email address submitted through a newsletter form or a message sent to our contact address. We may also collect basic technical information, including browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring URLs and general usage data.

Cookies and analytics

FinanceHub may use cookies, pixels or similar technologies to understand site performance, remember preferences and improve content. We may use Google Analytics or similar analytics tools to measure traffic and engagement. Visitors can adjust browser settings to block or delete cookies, though some site features may work differently.

Google AdSense and third-party advertising

FinanceHub may display advertising through Google AdSense or other third-party ad providers. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites. These cookies may support personalized advertising, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. Visitors can review Google's advertising settings and opt-out resources through Google's published privacy controls.

How information is used

We may use collected information to operate the site, improve content, respond to inquiries, understand reader interests, maintain security, measure advertising performance and comply with legal obligations.

Information sharing

We do not sell personal information directly to readers. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate analytics, advertising, hosting, email or security functions. These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

California privacy rights

California residents may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, including rights to know, delete, correct or opt out of certain uses of personal information. To submit a privacy request, contact us at contact@limafinancehub.com.

GDPR notice for EU visitors

Visitors from the European Economic Area or United Kingdom may have rights under GDPR or similar laws, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, but no website can guarantee absolute security. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here or as required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact FinanceHub at contact@limafinancehub.com.
